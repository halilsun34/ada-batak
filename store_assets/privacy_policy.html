Privacy Policy (English)

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Ada Batak ("Application") is an offline card game designed to provide an authentic, ad-free Batak experience. Protecting user privacy is our highest priority.

2. Data Collection & Sharing

Ada Batak DOES NOT COLLECT ANY PERSONAL DATA.

  • No internet connection is required or used by the app. Zero data is transmitted to external servers.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics.
  • There are NO third-party advertising SDKs and NO tracking/analytics libraries integrated into the app.

3. Local Storage

Game preferences (such as audio toggle, table theme, and game speed) are stored strictly on your local device storage. They never leave your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is completely safe for all audiences, including children. We do not solicit or gather any data from users of any age.
